## Images

All twenty-three website photographs are downloaded stock photographs from Pexels. Sources and the license page are recorded in photo-sources.json. WebP files are saved locally in public/images. No AI-generated photography is used. Nine former material photos were replaced with cleaner material displays, stacked goods, and organized tools. Four dedicated warehouse reference photographs show pallet storage, construction material racks, bulk timber, and material handling. The client approved using warehouse reference photographs pending real company images. These are prominently labeled as stock references, not company premises or verified inventory; no capacity, stock value, delivery guarantee, or facility ownership is invented. The vector logo is original code-native artwork, inspired by joined architectural profiles.

The seventeen material categories are proposed showcase directions within the documented business scope. Specific inventory, product brands, test results, delivery regions, and certifications are not established by the registration document. Visible third-party packaging in stock images does not establish an authorized dealership or stocked brand. Copy and reference labels avoid presenting stock photography as verified company projects or facilities. Eight categories appear in staggered, unequal image pairs on Home; phones use full-width stacked photos. The complete library uses three columns on desktop, two on tablet, and one on mobile. Home uses one warehouse hero and three different warehouse photographs below, without repeating a promotional photo. Image titles and inquiry copy are matched to the final photographs: metal sections are not mislabeled as rebar, and metal tubing is not mislabeled as plastic pipe.

## Design direction

Replaced on 2026-10-14 with a material-scale industrial identity. White and material charcoal; a restrained red action color; local Unbounded display typography and Onest text; a monumental name over warehouse reference photography; staggered product images; a dark warehouse spread; a native expandable supply process. Business facts and inquiry behavior are preserved.

Distinctive elements: the brand's MAX-scale wordmark, unequal material pairs, and the browser-local project brief. Decorative section numbers and the old green/serif chapter-index treatment were removed. The original architectural N logo is retained and adapted to the new palette and display wordmark.

Redesign references: Taste Skill's name-as-monument direction (seed 2135472913), Impeccable's new-work and craft-floor guidance, Nike's image-first typographic contrast, and Uber's clear neutral hierarchy from the local design-md library. No proprietary fonts, brand marks, or photography from those brands were copied. See DESIGN.md and docs/REDESIGN_QA.md for adaptations and executed checks.
